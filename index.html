A Global Cohort

The IBEA Program

The International Business Education Alliance (IBEA) is a cornerstone of my academic and professional journey. It is a unique, highly selective program that brings together 40 students-10 from each of four world-renowned business schools-for a two-year global immersion.

This unique cohort-based model fosters an incredibly strong global network and goes far beyond traditional academics. It's an education in adaptability, providing a tangible understanding of how business is conducted differently across the world's key economic blocs.
